# Florida Construction Pack

State depth for Florida, layered on top of the US Construction Pack and the US
cost data rather than repeating them. The national pack carries the
classification structures, the owner-contractor agreement family, the progress
payment application and imperial units. This pack carries what changes when a
job is in Florida.

## What this pack carries

### Sales tax and the county surtax

Under a lump sum, cost plus or guaranteed price contract the contractor is the
ultimate consumer of the materials it installs and pays tax when it buys them
(Rule 12A-1.051); the customer sees no tax line. Under a retail sale plus
installation contract the contractor collects tax from the customer on the
goods.

The state rate is 6 percent. Most counties add a discretionary surtax,
published each year on form DR-15DSS, and it reaches only the first 5,000
dollars of any single item (s. 212.054(2)(b)1). The county rate is an input;
the pack carries no county table.

Worked example, Miami-Dade at 1 percent: 450,000 dollars of materials, one of
them a 60,000 dollar chiller. State tax is 27,000; the surtax is 3,900 on the
bulk materials plus 50 on the chiller's first 5,000 dollars, 30,950 in all.

### Wages

Florida has no state prevailing wage law; Davis-Bacon applies where federal
money funds the work. From 30 September 2026 a political subdivision may not,
through its purchasing or contracting, seek to control the wages or benefits
its contractors provide (s. 218.077 as amended by ch. 2024-80). The amendment
does not impair a contract entered into before that date.

### Retainage

Local governments (s. 218.735(8)) and the state (s. 255.078) hold no more than
5 percent of each payment on a contract over 200,000 dollars. Private
retainage follows the contract; the balance is due within 14 days after
completion is certified (s. 715.12).

### Lien and payment clocks

- Notice to owner within 45 days of first furnishing, for lienors not in
  privity (s. 713.06(2)(a)). First furnishing on 10 March 2026 gives
  24 April 2026.
- Claim of lien within 90 days of final furnishing (s. 713.08(5)), served
  within 15 days of recording. Final furnishing on 20 October 2026 gives
  18 January 2027.
- The lien lapses one year after recording unless an action is started
  (s. 713.22(1)).
- Improvements of 2,500 dollars or less are outside the notice machinery
  (s. 713.02(5)).
- Local government payments are due 20 business days after the request is
  stamped received (s. 218.735(1)).
