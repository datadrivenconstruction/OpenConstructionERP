# Monte Carlo confidence level and photo albums

## Choose the confidence level of a risk run

1. Open **Risk > Monte Carlo**.
2. Set **Confidence level (P)**, a whole number from 50 to 99, usually 80. At P80, 80 of every 100 simulated outcomes cost no more than the figure. A higher level gives a larger reserve.
3. For risks with a single figure, enter a low and a high value to give the simulation a range.
4. Run the simulation. The result shows P90 and your chosen level. The settings are stored with the last run and come back when you reopen the tab.

## Keep category and album in the photo drawer

1. Open **Photos** and a photo.
2. Choose the **Category** from the shared set and an **Album**, or **No album**. A photo in several albums lists them.
3. The change is saved and read back, and the drawer confirms it. If the project has no album yet, create one under **Albums** first.

Viewers see the controls disabled, with the reason.
