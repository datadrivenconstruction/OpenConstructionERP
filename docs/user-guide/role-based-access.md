# Roles: actions you cannot use say why

Every role can open and read the screens of its project. Actions the role may not perform stay visible but disabled, and the tooltip tells you which role is needed.

1. Open any screen, for example a purchase order, the cost model or a register.
2. Look for a greyed-out button or field. Hover or focus it: the tooltip reads, for example, that the action is for editors, managers or admins.
3. Ask a project admin for a higher role, or have someone with that role do the step.

The same rule applies to read-only previews: a preview needs only the read permission, and the step that commits the change keeps the update permission. Screens that already work this way include finance, procurement, contracts, equipment, assemblies, the BOQ templates and catalog, site logistics, the survey plan, clash detection and accounting sync. Import buttons show the arrow into the tray and export buttons the arrow out, on all screens.
