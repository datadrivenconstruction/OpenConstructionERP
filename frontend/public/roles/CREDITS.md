# Role portraits

`<role>-96.webp` and `<role>-192.webp` are square face crops of the portraits already shipped
in this repository under `frontend/public/assets/people/prf-*.webp` (the project's own cast
of international construction specialists). No third-party or stock images were added, so
there is no extra licence to carry. Mapping: see `ROLE_META` ids in
`frontend/src/features/cases/roles.ts`; each crop is the top 340x340 region of one portrait.
